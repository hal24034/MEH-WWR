Professional Detailing. Exceptional Results.

At Apex Auto Detailing, we believe every vehicle deserves proper care. Our detailing services are designed to improve the appearance of your vehicle while helping maintain its condition. We offer several packages to make it easy to find the right service for your needs. If you have any questions or need special treatment reach out to us and we’ll see what we can do!

Available Services:

Interior Detailing

Our interior detailing service focuses on refreshing the inside of your vehicle. This includes vacuuming, cleaning carpets and upholstery, wiping down interior surfaces, and cleaning windows. - $200-$300 (Depending on vehicle size)

Exterior Detailing

Our exterior detailing service focuses on giving your vehicle a clean, polished appearance. Services include hand washing, wheel and tire cleaning, exterior surface cleaning, and finishing. $100-$200 (Depending on vehicle size)

Complete Detailing

Our complete detailing package combines interior and exterior services for a thorough vehicle refresh. It is ideal for customers who want their entire vehicle cleaned and refreshed. $400-$600 (Depending on vehicle size)
